# Wrongful termination of a construction or EPC contract, parties, and forum

Apply the termination sections when the employer has sent a termination notice, retendered the work,
or handed it to another contractor. Apply the parties and forum sections to every construction
complaint when fixing the caption and the addressee court.

## 1. Test the employer's termination

**Trigger.** The employer sent a notice terminating the whole or part of the contract, or took the work
away by retendering or reassigning it.

**Operation.**
1. Did the contract or the law entitle the employer to terminate on these facts? Check the
   termination clause, its conditions (notice, cure period, the kind of breach), and whether those
   conditions happened on the documents. If not, the notice does not terminate the contract, and the
   employer's conduct is a breach, usually fundamental.
2. Because the notice was ineffective, the contract is still on foot. Damages for loss of the bargain
   presuppose termination: request termination by the court first, and state the effective date the
   court should fix (commonly the date the complaint is served on the defendant), and the scope of the
   termination your documents support.
3. Never concede, anywhere in the complaint, that the contract ended on the date of the notice, and do
   not anchor any start date on the notice.

**Not applicable** where the employer did have a termination right; then the claim is for settlement of
what was performed.

## 2. Money after a wrongful termination

**Operation.**
- **Losses, not price.** Costs incurred for work the wrongful termination cut off (design completed,
  temporary works, mobilisation, procurement) are claimed as losses caused by the breach, not as price
  under the payment clauses, so their recovery and interest do not hang on payment conditions that will
  never be met.
- **Allocation.** Where your own side's evidence already allocates a shared cost to the terminated part,
  use that allocation as it stands; do not build your own allocation key from contract prices.
- **Lost benefit.** The benefit the plaintiff would have obtained from performance, limited to what the
  employer could foresee at formation, is claimed with its amount if the inputs fix it, or with a bare
  marker. Never compute it from an assumed margin or percentage.
- **Charge for use of funds.** Money the employer should have paid and has kept runs a charge for the
  use of funds, starting when termination takes effect, at the market lending reference rate where no
  rate was agreed. Cite, with its article number, the current judicial-interpretation provision on the
  charge for use of funds after a contract is terminated, next to this request.
- **Price for work performed and accepted.** A part completed and settled before termination is claimed
  as price under section 3 of `references/construction-price-and-priority.md`.

**Check.** Termination is requested before loss of bargain; each loss head has a base or marker; the
charge for use of funds starts at the effective date of termination and cites the current provision.

## 3. Parties

- **Plaintiff.** The party that contracted for the works and holds the claim now: the contractor
  against the employer, the subcontractor against the main contractor, the designer-builder against the
  owner. Check the direction the instruction gives.
- **The employer.** The contracting party named in the contract, with its particulars as the inputs
  give them, including particulars found only in a stamp, a registration extract, or an evidence list.
- **A head company for its branch.** A branch has no separate liability; join the head company when the
  contract was signed by a branch, and plead the rule.
- **The sole shareholder of a one-person company.** It is jointly liable for the company's debts unless
  it proves its own property is separate from the company's. Join it when the documents show the
  employer has a single shareholder and who it is, and plead the rule; the burden sits on the
  shareholder.
- **A person who owes a request under a document.** A guarantor who signed, a joint payer named in the
  same undertaking.
- **Leave out** the supervisor, the cost consultant, a later contractor, an
  affiliate that ran the tender, and anyone who only appears in the facts. The complaint does not say who
  was not sued.

Every defendant needs, in the facts and reasons, the fact and the rule that make it liable, and at least
one request that orders it to do something.

## 4. Forum for construction disputes

Work through these in your notes; the result appears only as the court after `此致`.

1. **Arbitration.** An arbitration clause binding both parties on this relation takes the dispute out
   of court. If there is none, arbitration is not mentioned.
2. **Specialised courts.** A dispute over a construction contract for railway works (lines, sidings,
   stations) is heard by the railway transport court for that area, whatever the
   parties agreed. Check whether the works fall in such a category before looking at any agreed court.
3. **Exclusive jurisdiction.** Disputes over construction contracts are heard where the works are
   located. An agreed forum elsewhere, or the defendant's domicile, does not displace this.
4. **Level.** The amount in dispute is the sum of the amounts actually stated in the requests; a head
   carried only by a marker adds nothing to it. Address the level the local thresholds give for that
   amount; when the inputs do not let you apply a threshold, address the basic-level court of the place.
   Do not raise the level on the strength of a figure you did not request.
5. **Name.** If the inputs identify the court only by the place of the works, write the court of that
   place at that level, using as much of the place name as the inputs provide and a bare marker for the
   missing part.

## 5. Self-check

1. Where the employer terminated, its right to do so was tested, and termination by the court is
   requested before any loss of bargain.
2. Costs of work cut off are claimed as losses; any allocation is your own evidence's; no margin is
   assumed.
3. Every defendant owes a request under a stated rule; nobody is joined for appearing in the facts.
4. The forum analysis ran in order in your notes, checking specialised and exclusive jurisdiction before
   any agreed court, and the court appears only in the closing.
