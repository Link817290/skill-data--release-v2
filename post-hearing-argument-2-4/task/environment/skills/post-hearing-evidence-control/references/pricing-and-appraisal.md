# Valuation And Appraisal Route

Use this route whenever the record contains an expert figure, excluded cost, schedule rate, proposed total, or market comparator.

## Figure register

Create one row per figure with: valued object, work scope, valuation date or version, method, proponent, source, included and excluded components, evidentiary status, and requested treatment. Freeze the register before drafting. Two figures that use different scopes or authority levels are not competing totals until those differences are reconciled.

Classify each figure as one of: contractual, expert-determinable, expert-disputed, party-proposed, market-comparator, or judicially adopted. Never promote a value to a stronger class because it is precise or repeated.

## Exclusion analysis

For every disputed cost or profit component, answer these independently:

1. Is there an express agreement about inclusion or exclusion?
2. What work, organization, burden, or risk is the component said to compensate?
3. Does the available valuation actually include or exclude it, and on what stated premise?
4. Is exclusion argued from personal qualification, lack of actual work, lack of proof, prior payment, or a valuation rule?
5. Does any cited decision share the material factual conditions, or is it only an analogy?

Do not collapse qualification, actual performance, valuation method, and evidentiary burden into one conclusion. A personal-status objection does not by itself answer whether work was performed or a component was already carried elsewhere.

## Verification

Before finalizing, trace each requested valuation to one figure-register row. If the evidence cannot establish a final sum, state what the court or expert must determine and why. The requested disposition must recognize proved work, preserve genuinely disputed components, and keep party proposals and market comparators distinct from expert-certified or judicially adopted values.
