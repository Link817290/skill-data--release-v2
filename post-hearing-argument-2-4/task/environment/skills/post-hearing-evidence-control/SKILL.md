# Post-Hearing Evidence Control

Use this method when the record contains competing accounts of construction price, payment, authority, or responsibility. Read every supplied source before drafting. The method is a control for finding and testing propositions; it never supplies a missing fact or legal conclusion.

## Five-pass workflow

1. **Map the dispute.** Make one private row for each claim, defense, offset, counterclaim, and requested remedy. Record the business object, proponent, source location, contrary account, proof status, and requested consequence. Do not merge rows merely because they concern the same project or party.
2. **Reconcile every number.** Make a separate row for each valuation, transfer, wage payment, offset, principal, and interest component. Record payer, recipient, covered work, period, proof, asserted legal effect, and whether another row overlaps it. Calculate and explain discrepancies between competing totals; never just list the totals or choose one silently.
3. **Classify valuation authority.** For every price figure, distinguish contract price, expert-determinable amount, disputed expert component, party proposal, market comparator, and court finding. For every excluded cost, identify the asserted exclusion ground separately: express agreement, actual work, valuation method, qualification, proof of burden, or prior payment. Use [pricing-and-appraisal.md](references/pricing-and-appraisal.md) for this pass.
4. **Trace authority and knowledge.** For every third-party payment, worker account, signature, or witness statement, trace identity, act, authority for that act, ratification, and transaction-specific consequence. A signature or site role is not itself financial authority, and direct knowledge of work is not knowledge of another party's contract. Use [payment-and-authority.md](references/payment-and-authority.md) for this pass.
5. **Draft and close.** Write one bounded issue unit for each material row: opposing proposition, supporting record, adopted analysis, any necessary qualification, and requested disposition. Advocacy should state the strongest supported conclusion; uncertainty is not a substitute for analysis. Finish with a reverse audit from every requested remedy back to its calculation, evidence, and issue unit.

## Artifact and consistency checks

Inspect body paragraphs, tables, headers, footers, text boxes, and other OOXML text carriers before making an exclusionary statement. Mark an unreadable carrier as unavailable rather than absent. Then verify:

- every competing total has an explicit reconciliation or an identified unresolved difference;
- every appraisal or market figure keeps its correct author and authority status;
- every disputed payment has a non-overlapping allocation and a supported authorization path;
- every alleged signature is tested for scope and ratification, not only authenticity;
- the final requested valuation recognizes proved work and addresses disputed components without presenting a party proposal as adjudicated;
- the narrative, calculations, and requested remedies use the same adopted figures and statuses.

Stop and label the issue unresolved when the conclusion depends on an unavailable original, an unproved relationship, or a calculation that cannot be reproduced. Do not turn a party assertion into a finding, replace a missing value with zero, or mention this skill, its private ledgers, or evaluation language in the deliverable.
