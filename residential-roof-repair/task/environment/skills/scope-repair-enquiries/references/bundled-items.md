# Preserve the work inside composite items

For each selected line, read every numbered characteristic. Record material, thickness, grade, layer sequence, termination height, accessories and included preparatory work. The quantity and rate belong to that described bundle, not merely its short item title.

If a membrane line includes a concrete levelling layer, retain the layer in the enquiry description. A separate breakdown can be acceptable for price transparency only if it reconciles to the same included work and avoids charging for it twice. Do not silently replace a specified layer with a generic substrate-preparation allowance.

Accessory counts are distinct from pipe length. Keep their type and count with the parent drainage item; adding counts to metres is dimensionally invalid. A diameter alone does not establish pipe material. The material of a hopper or outlet does not establish the material of the entire pipe run.

Specifications remain local to their stated item. A facade insulation fire rating or conductivity limit must not migrate to a roof insulation item without evidence. When specifications are incomplete, request confirmation instead of promoting an assumption to a contractual requirement.

Avoid deriving material purchase volumes directly from measured surface quantities that may include vertical returns or different layer extents. Such estimates need a compatible measurement basis, geometry and any documented waste allowance.
