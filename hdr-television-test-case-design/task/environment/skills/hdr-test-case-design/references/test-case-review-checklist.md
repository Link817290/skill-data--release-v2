# HDR Test Case Review Checklist

Review each test case as one closed unit.

## Objective and applicability

- The Test Module and Test Case Name describe the same HDR function.
- The device material supports that function.
- The case has one primary objective; unrelated measurements are separated.

## Preconditions

- Required physical connection and equipment are stated.
- The necessary environment and TV state are stated.
- Required signal, source, format, or picture mode is available.
- Preconditions do not assume an unsupported device or instrument capability.

## Test steps

- The sequence begins with a safe, reproducible setup and readiness check.
- Signal or pattern configuration appears before transmission or measurement.
- Controlled changes identify what stays constant and what changes.
- Observation or measurement actions identify the quantity or behavior being reviewed.
- The tester is told what to record or compare.

## Expected result

- Every expected result is caused or observed by a corresponding step.
- A numeric value retains its unit, direction, and comparison boundary.
- Subjective picture-quality language is paired with an observable artifact or comparison.
- No expected result silently changes the HDR format, operating mode, or test condition.

## Whole-workbook closure

- Supported HDR functions are covered without duplicate objectives.
- Every project acceptance-table relationship is traceable to a stimulus, measurement or observation, and expected decision.
- Splitting or combining rows has not caused any acceptance relationship to disappear.
- Column meanings and terminology stay consistent.
- Test Result and Remark remain separate from the designed Expected Result.
- No setup boilerplate hides a missing case-specific action.
