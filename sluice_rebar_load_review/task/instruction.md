# Structural calculation review

A design office must decide whether the structural calculations in a water-control-gate rehabilitation report are ready for approval. Review the river-sluice design subsection and the serviceability provisions it relies on, covering load combinations, stability checks, reinforcement evidence, crack widths, and deflections.

The report under `/workspace/materials` is the sole evidence source and must remain unmodified. Deliver `/workspace/review.xlsx` in English, with one traceable row for every check, a resolved status (`PASS`, `FAIL`, or `REVIEW`), the observed value or relationship, the governing criterion, a report locator, and a concise finding. Keep a conclusion at `REVIEW` when a binding input is absent, and retain spreadsheet formulas for derived arithmetic or reinforcement-area values. Include an overall approval disposition that distinguishes a reported comparison from an independently reproducible calculation chain, and link each approval blocker to the affected check and the evidence needed to close it.

Before beginning, apply any review procedures available in the environment whose stated scope fits this calculation book.

The workbook is complete when the approval-relevant findings in that structural scope can be audited from the supplied report alone.
