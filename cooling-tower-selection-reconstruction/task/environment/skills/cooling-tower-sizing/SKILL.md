# Cooling Tower Sizing

Use this method when constructing a thermal and fan-sizing workbook from engineering inputs and empirical design data.

Define physical boundaries and quantity bases before building the model.
Separate moist-air properties, transfer demand, available packing performance
and equipment requirements; each describes a different part of the design.

Use conservation relationships alongside the supplied empirical design data.
Distinguish physical relationships from correlations whose coefficients depend
on particular units or applicability conditions.

Evaluate candidates consistently and carry the adopted candidate into the
downstream model. Separate candidate feasibility from the adequacy of installed
equipment, allowing an unsuccessful design condition to remain visible.

This supplies a modeling approach without task formulas or a selected answer.
Use `spreadsheet-behavior-checks` for generic workbook inspection and recalculation.
