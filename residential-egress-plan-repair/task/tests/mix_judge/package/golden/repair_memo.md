## Scope
The review covers the first-floor `HALL-ROUTE-01` and the north opening associated with `HALL-DOOR-01` in the supplied plan. Native drawing measurements are in inches; rule comparisons are reported in metres.

## Findings and disposition
- `F-HALL-WIDTH`: the source route is 40.000 in (1.016 m). The drawing shows a coordination concept that removes the existing lower face and relocates a parallel replacement face to provide 1.100 m clear width, with proposed end connections. Wall thickness, structure/services and adjacent-room effects remain design-coordination items; this is not an as-built completion claim.
- `F-HALL-DOOR`: north A-OPENING handle `165D` is a 36.000 in (0.9144 m) plan opening line. It does not establish installed clear width after jamb, leaf and hardware deductions, and the supplied record does not establish that this internal opening is a dwelling entrance or safety exit within the cited 0.900 m threshold. Classification and effective clear width remain on coordination hold.
- `F-HALL-SWING`: the supplied plan does not establish a swing arc for north opening `165D`. This remains a separate review hold; no unsupported swing geometry is drawn.
