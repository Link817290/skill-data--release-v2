# Wedding Truss Safety Procedure Rubric

## Authority and Measurement

The executable authority is `task/tests/test.sh`, the program judge and the
source-bound mixed contract. Deterministic checks own document structure, required
stages and observable safety relations. The semantic judge owns professional
equivalents split across lifecycle sections. Infrastructure errors, invalid judge
responses and replay misses are not candidate zeroes.

The strict score is the weighted arithmetic mean across fourteen tagged business owners. Independent chains within an owner average. A declared dependent chain uses `min`; adopted unsafe statements zero only the affected owner once. There is no global exponent, curvature, or piecewise amplification.

## Subability tags

`venue-protection`, `span-support`, `height-stability`, `ballast-control`, `incoming-readiness`, `credential-integrity`, `assembly-dependencies`, `electrical-installation`, `life-safety`, `joint-acceptance`, `clean-handover`, `ceremony-response`, `stable-dismantling`, `chinese-carrier`.

The independent lenient score requires incoming inspection and evidence integrity
(P04a-P04c), the five-stage control chain represented by heavy-structure sequencing,
electrical commissioning, joint release, clean handover and stable dismantling
(P05a, P06c, P08a, P08d and P10b), a Chinese carrier (G02a), no adopted safety error,
and a clean required deliverable. Details and exact weights are authoritative in
`task/tests/rubric-contract.json`.

## Weight Rationale

The fourteen owners separate the actual safety lifecycle: venue protection,
structural support, height and ballast stability, inspection, assembly, electrical
work, life safety, release, ceremony response, dismantling and handover. The
weighted mean gives each responsibility a visible contribution without a
document-wide multiplier. Dependent relations use `min` only when both parts are
genuinely required for the same safety outcome. The lenient lens keeps incoming
inspection, the five-stage control chain, the Chinese carrier, integrity and a
clean deliverable as minimum usability conditions.
