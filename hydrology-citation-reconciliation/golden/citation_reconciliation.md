### F01 - Author decision required

- Category: Cited-only work
- Location: printed p. 4592, opening lines (physical p. 4)
- Observed item: `Bergstrom and Forsman, 1973`
- Counterpart: No Bergstrom/Forsman 1973 entry appears on reference-list pp. 4605-4607.

- Recommended disposition: Ask the authors to supply the full 1973 record or remove/replace the callout; do not infer the missing metadata.
