# Scoring

Strict treats the measured professional owners as one integrated finished-film
delivery: story, performance, picture, sound and reproducible source must work
together. Failed checks retain their loss, and damage observations do not add a
duplicate penalty. The same evidenced framing obstruction failing both P07
diagnostics is counted once after semantic ownership review.

This integrated view reflects ordinary post-production review. A technically
playable export can still be materially incomplete when its reveal is unclear,
performances are miscast, captions are unreadable, the mix fights the reaction,
or the project cannot reproduce the delivered cut. Small independent shortfalls
can also combine into a weaker finished experience, so strict evaluates their
joint effect rather than treating strong craft in one department as full
compensation for missing work in another. Each underlying check remains visible
and receives only its observed partial completion; the settlement adds no hidden
aggregate verdict beyond the observed evidence.

The published settlement applies a bounded, monotone joint-quality curve to the
nine non-substitutable owners listed below. A perfect owner vector remains exactly
1; partial deficiencies across story, performance, picture, dialogue, encoded
media, editable source and sound combine in the finished-film experience. The
curve is an evaluator implementation detail: the public rubric explains the
professional dependency and local evidence, while the machine-readable evaluator
is the sole authority for numeric settlement. Calibration constants are not part
of the actor-facing task materials.

If reproducible source and plan completion is below one, the integrated result
also reflects two remaining-value losses: the delivered cut is harder to
reproduce, and future timing, caption, compositing and sound corrections are
harder to make.
The underlying N07 owner already records the local source defect; the multiplier
records its cross-department production consequence. Missing and wrong source
work receive their observed P11 completion rather than a fixed cap. Other failed
checks retain only their owner loss. Repeated evidence of one P07 framing cause
is merged before owner aggregation, so it is not charged twice.

| Requirement | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| Workday, overtime, and next-morning story | P01/P03/P04 |
| Trivial-edit expectation and complete-rework reveal | P02 |
| Specific supplied performers and developing performance | P05 |
| Keying, composition, pacing, shot changes, visual system, graphic devices | P06/P07/P09/P13/P14/P15 |
| Readable attributed Simplified Chinese dialogue | P08 |
| Playable required-format delivery | P12 |
| Reproducible project and truthful complete timeline | P11 |
| Meaningful original role voices and correct narrative assignment | Five source cue verdicts |
| Intentional soundtrack and clean encoded signal | P10 and soundtrack design |

Each semantic P item has independently evidenced checks and can receive partial
completion; P11 combines real render correspondence and semantic plan fidelity.
Role cues are the source boss introduction and rework explanation, puzzled voice,
shocked voice, and mocking laughter. Fixed source waveforms are objectively
matched; recognizable identity, meaningful original vocal expression, narrative
placement and actual routing require source/candidate-bound semantic evidence.
Each voice earns credit only when its identity, meaningful expression and beat
are preserved. Private reference crop lengths and unique energy fractions are
diagnostic only. Ordinary gain changes, silence removal and reasonable editorial
trims do not lose credit; actual replacement, harmful truncation of key expression,
or wrong emotional/narrative assignment does. All voice references come from issued media.

The two soundtrack design checks assess intentional narrative support and
placement/mixing for repeated work and reversal. These accept music changes,
equivalent designed cues, and purposeful silence. No synthetic Golden effect
recipe, exact frequency, fixed effect count, or absolute timestamp is mandatory.
Actual decoded signal and source routing evidence accompany the judgment;
signal loudness alone does not establish intentional design.

Lenient is the conjunction of nine independent minimum-usability predicates:
valid technical media, reproducible project, truthful timeline, complete core
story, intended cast, developing performance, readable dialogue, required visual
devices, and audible sound. It is not derived from strict score and does not
certify full original-voice fidelity, intentional soundtrack design, perfect edge
keying, visual style, or a fixed source-audio extent; those remain strict quality
requirements.

Strict and lenient therefore answer different questions. Strict measures the
professional completeness of the integrated film and project. Lenient asks only
whether the delivery remains usable: valid media, reproducible source, truthful
plan, complete core story, intended cast, developing performance, readable
dialogue, required visual devices and audible sound. A strict shortfall does not
automatically fail lenient, and lenient is never inferred from a strict threshold.

Equivalent framing/encoding of the same source performer, equivalent graphic
design and pacing, captions preserving dialogue meaning, and equivalent designed
effects are accepted. Different performances with merely broad similar emotion
labels do not replace the specific screenplay performances. The instruction's
explicit role-source map controls any generic skill advice; the screenplay
controls narrative order, relationships and emotional development.

Invalid or unplayable submitted media is a measured artifact failure and scores
zero. Tool, runtime, API, malformed judgment, unresolved evidence, and absent or
invalid replay-cache failures are UNMEASURED infrastructure outcomes: nonzero
exit, detailed diagnostic, and no numeric reward. Successful production
`reward.json` contains only `strict` and `lenient`; check evidence, partial-credit
owners, deduplication and damage attribution remain in `reward_detail.json`.
