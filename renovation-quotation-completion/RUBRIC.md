# Quotation Scoring Rubric

Strict reward is the arithmetic sum of four weighted business owners. Within an owner, a `min` conjunction is used only when every listed prerequisite jointly establishes the same source-supported quotation result. Independent owners retain weighted partial credit. There is no global gamma, power, curvature, completeness multiplier, fixed global deduction, or piecewise amplification.

The mixed strict score is `0.80 * program quotation score + 0.20 * source-bound semantic score`. The program layer owns source inputs, formulas, arithmetic closure and uncertainty-safe subtotals. The semantic layer owns open professional meaning: whether unresolved inputs remain conditional, excluded or pending rather than being presented as a completed fixed quotation, and whether scope and status remain faithful to the supplied evidence. Direct weighting keeps independent arithmetic and professional-meaning evidence visible; neither layer is centered, curved, capped or specially overridden for Golden.

| ID | Weight | Owner |
|---|---:|---|
| O01 | 0.10 | Supplied quantity, rate, unit and scope inputs remain unchanged. |
| O02 | 0.40 | Supported main-material closure: four local section chains, installed equipment 2 x 450 = 900, and main totals all reconcile. |
| O03 | 0.05 | Six supported total dependency trees, each independently replayed. |
| O04 | 0.45 | Nine foundation sections; each local chain requires supported detail calculations, safe unresolved-input treatment and its own subtotal closure. |

Unresolved inputs may remain explicitly unpriced/excluded or match the unchanged authoritative reference; invented adopted amounts do not satisfy the chain. Affirmative errors are recorded once under the owner whose chain already reflects the error, so they do not double-charge completed work.

The independent lenient result requires one native workbook, complete preservation of supplied source inputs, substantive supported pricing coverage, and at least half of the independent total dependency trees to reconcile. An unchanged blank source workbook cannot pass. It is not derived from the strict score.
