# Renovation Quotation

Work in the supplied editable schedule. Identify the active project and distinguish
it from completed examples before calculating. Keep the existing business scope,
units and source values traceable, including when a drawing suggests a different
quantity. A drawing may cover only part of a multi-level or exterior scope.

For each price section, establish what its column headings mean and classify the
detail rows before filling amounts. Read [pricing-regimes](references/pricing-regimes.md)
when labor components, equipment or material losses use different conventions.
Use [scope-and-closure](references/scope-and-closure.md) when subtotals cross section
boundaries, source quantities conflict, or a commercial input is unavailable.

Keep calculations in the workbook with formulas or auditable numeric results.
Finish supported detail calculations before closing their subtotals. Reconcile
section totals to the final quotation without counting both a subtotal and its
children. If the quote cannot be final, distinguish the supported subtotal from
unresolved amounts and explain the uncertainty beside the affected rows.

Reopen the saved workbook to check that amounts, formulas and output cells remain
readable. Use consistent currency precision and preserve useful source formatting.
An additional narrative report is not a substitute for the editable quotation.

Stop assigning final amounts when quantity, rate or commercial approval is missing.
Do not infer an agreed price from a superficially similar item in another project,
or convert an example total into a new price basis.
