# Recovery and release boundaries

Tie each exception to an observation and a supported recovery. Separate sensing,
position, format-scale and output-quality failures; changing an offset may hide a
symptom without correcting the cause. Recheck the affected quality after recovery.

When a requested job is infeasible, explain the controlling bound and the decision
needed. Prepare conditional options without presenting them as authorized setup.
A smaller output, a different machine or an added accessory changes the job and
requires the corresponding business decision.

Treat catalogue start values as start values. A maximum temperature, force, speed
or darkness is not automatically the best corrective setting. Use only adjustments
supported for the applicable model and pursue a verified operating result.

An operational document can remain useful with pending fields: mark actual trial
settings, measurements and confirmation for completion at the appropriate step.
Do not fill them from an assumed ideal result.
