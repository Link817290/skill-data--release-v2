# Site works takeoff from a round figure

Use this when the survey record gives cabling, containment or civil work as a single figure.

## Why a round figure is hard to quote

A single figure hides how far things run, what each run needs, and what a unit of work costs. The customer cannot test it and the crew cannot build to it, and when the site differs from the survey there is no agreed way to adjust it.

## Taking it apart

1. From the photographs and notes, identify what the figure physically pays for: the kinds of cable, the containment they run in, any work to surfaces or structures they cross, and any bases or fixings.
2. Estimate each quantity from the layout: where things start and end, how many runs, and a sensible allowance for routing. Record the basis of each estimate in terms of the site.
3. Sanity check each quantity against the size of the site.

## Rates

* If house rates are given, use them.
* If only the round figure is given, you may derive rates so that the parts reproduce it, and say so. Do not introduce an amount the record and the house rules do not support.

## Settlement

Tell the customer how the final quantities will be agreed.
