# Task Instruction

You handle estimating at a parking systems integrator in Qingdao. A colleague spent this morning at a residential compound scoping an entrance barrier and plate recognition job. What came back is in `materials/`: two photographs of the entrances, and a photograph of the sheet he wrote while he was on site.

Turn that into the formal quotation we issue to the customer. Save it as an Excel workbook at `output/quotation.xlsx`.

The customer reads this document to decide whether to sign, and our own installers work from it once the job starts, so it has to stand on its own after the scoping sheet is no longer attached to it. The sheet is our record of what the site needs.

House rules that apply to every quotation we issue:

1. Unit prices are the ones settled on site.
2. We do not bill installation to the customer as a lump sum. Transport and commissioning is charged at 10 percent, maintenance at another 10 percent, both calculated on the total of everything quoted before those two charges.
3. Our prices are inclusive of a general VAT invoice at 1 percent.
4. Date the quotation 2026.7.21.

We move quantities around during negotiation, so the line amounts and the totals need to recalculate inside the workbook rather than sit there as typed in numbers.
