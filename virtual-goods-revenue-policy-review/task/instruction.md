# Longitudinal revenue-recognition policy and control review

You are the audit manager performing the year-end review of an issuer's revenue-recognition workpaper after a registration-stage policy revision and restatement, subsequent control remediation, and later estimate changes. Using only the materials supplied under `/workspace`, complete the header-only `review_template.csv` in English with one separately supportable accounting or control conclusion per row. Cover every material revenue-recognition position, version conflict, unresolved evidentiary point, and related financial-reporting or control consequence from the registration record through the latest supplied annual report. State both the conclusion at each relevant reporting date and its status at the latest review date. Do not use the network, do not modify the supplied source files, and do not state more certainty than the supplied record supports.

Save the completed ledger as `/workspace/revenue_policy_review.csv`. Each row must contain a finding identifier, topic, conclusion, accounting effect, rationale, pinpoint evidence naming the source file and numbered comment, note, page, or section, residual evidence needed (or an explicit statement that none is needed), and the audit response. The review is complete when another audit reviewer can trace each conclusion across the record and can distinguish historical assertions, management conclusions, audited policy and estimate disclosures, and external-control assurance.
