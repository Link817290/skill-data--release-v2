# Filing table controls

When a disclosure has a crowded or split table, capture the following fields before doing arithmetic:

| Control | What to capture | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Period identity | fiscal year end and comparative years | prevents mixing adjacent periods |
| Population | aggregate, plan, or named employee | prevents false reconciliation |
| Unit scale | exact shares, thousands, or millions | prevents ten- or million-fold errors |
| Movement sign | additions versus deductions | preserves conservation arithmetic |
| Performance basis | target, expected, or maximum | prevents double counting |
| Withholding treatment | net exercise or tax shares | explains differences between event and issued shares |
| Capacity basis | reserved, available, or reissuable | separates pool capacity from awards outstanding |

For each captured fact, retain a locator that is meaningful inside the supplied file. The locator is evidence metadata, not a substitute for the value.
