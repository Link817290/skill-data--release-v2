# Match conclusion strength to evidence

Use a small claim ladder:

1. Observed/reported value, identified by event and method.
2. Recalculation conditional on stated inputs.
3. Numerical comparison conditional on criterion applicability.
4. Valid scoped monitoring conclusion after method and operating evidence checks.
5. Overall dossier readiness after material obligations and open issues are closed.

Do not jump from level2 to an acceptance decision. Separate a source statement
from the reviewer's endorsement, especially in an annotated draft that must keep
the original assertions visible.

## Write useful comments

Attach the comment to the assertion, row or cell it addresses. Include the
specific inconsistency or calculation, its consequence for the claimed conclusion,
and the primary record or authorized correction that could resolve it. One
comment may group linked issues if its anchor and closure instructions remain
clear. There is no universal required number of comments.

Use three dispositions:

- Supported within scope: retain the numerical comparison with date and boundary.
- Confirmed documentary inconsistency: preserve observations and request a
  traceable correction against the originating record.
- Insufficient evidence: withhold only the affected claim and request the
  material evidence capable of changing it.

Ask first for an existing relevant record rather than automatically ordering
new measurements. If the record cannot establish validity or sampling was
unsuitable, identify the necessary targeted additional work. Do not demand a
complete new campaign merely because a summary lacks an attachment. Conversely,
a correct arithmetic check cannot replace missing material sampling evidence.

## Close business decisions

Identify the record owner and what completion means: a corrected calculation,
confirmed asset identity, contemporaneous operating evidence, an applicable
criterion, or a supported classification/destination. Do not invent an approval,
deadline, inspection, corrective action already completed or authorized sign-off.

Waste descriptions must be linked to origin, composition and actual route.
Conflicting ordinary/hazardous classifications require supporting determination;
do not assign an invented code or authorize resale from a convenient paragraph.
An absent permit, contract or transfer record in the working dossier does not
prove it never existed.

In the summary opinion, prioritize material blockers and preserve supported
findings. State whether review can proceed conditionally or what prevents the
affected conclusion. Keep the assessed project phase and historical date clear.
A document reviewer does not replace the responsible entity's statutory decision
or a competent site inspection.

## Saved-document check

Read native Word comments and anchors after saving. A plain paragraph extractor
can miss every comment and falsely treat the retained flawed draft as the
reviewer's conclusion. Confirm each comment points to the intended issue;
preserve measured table values and original narrative when annotation is
required. Review original and supplemental opinion together for contradictions.
Formatting and successful extraction are not evidence that the business
reasoning is correct; perform both checks separately.
