# Review dimensions

The deterministic part checks actual native comments, valid anchors, preservation of the public draft and a separate readable opinion. The semantic part evaluates evidence-based business conclusions, not phrases or visual similarity.

Accept equivalent comments and justified alternative calculations. The draft is evidence to critique, not an answer key. Do not reward blind agreement with its conformity statements. Sources with absent limits, censored values or absent supporting records require distinct dispositions.

Primary ownership: identity/date/investment -> consistency; rate/arithmetic/ND/no-limit/noise -> measurement; historical applicability/paired efficiency/annual extrapolation -> applicability; waste classification/closure/acceptance recommendation -> disposition. Do not deduct one causal mistake across multiple dimensions.

Strict reward first computes the published weighted sum of four responsibility owners in
`task/tests/scoring_params.json`: dossier consistency (20%), measurement
interpretation (30%), criterion applicability (30%), and evidence-bounded
disposition (20%). Each owner's reported completion is the arithmetic mean of
its independent checks. This reflects professional review:
one incomplete observation reduces the responsibility in proportion to its
scope instead of erasing other correct measurements in the same domain.

The breadth score is then harmonically balanced with the mean completion of
four source-derived critical controls: polymer-load reconciliation, censored
non-detect handling, paired fume-efficiency evaluation, and laboratory-stack
limit derating. These controls are the places where a superficially complete
review can still reverse the acceptance decision or apply the wrong limit. The
harmonic balance requires both broad review coverage and competent handling of
those decision-critical evidence transformations; unlike a minimum, one
partial control does not erase unrelated correct work, and unlike a plain
average, broad low-risk observations cannot fully compensate for missing
critical analysis. The separate lenient lens still requires every
responsibility to reach its minimum usable level.

Laboratory-stack review combines source-bound semantic review with a
deterministic check of the stack IDs, height condition, base rate and requested
recalculation; the higher supported completion is used. It must distinguish
DA001-DA012 and their derating evidence from the wastewater-station 14 kg/h
record; isolated numbers, fixed phrases and action keywords do not establish
the required conclusion.

Lenient reward requires every responsibility owner and each of the four
decision-critical controls to reach at least half completion. This tests
minimum operational usability without converting a single fully correct item
into a pass for an otherwise incomplete owner or allowing broad low-risk
coverage to substitute for a missing load, censored-value, paired-efficiency,
or stack-derating decision.


Causal ownership: oil-fume concentration selection, removal efficiency, supporting evidence and recommendations directly derived from that evaluation belong exclusively to `applicability`. Do not deduct them again under `measurement` or `disposition`. A general release decision independent of that single topic remains within `disposition`.
