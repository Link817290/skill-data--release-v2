# Prepare the slope-support tender bill of quantities

The procurement team needs a bill of quantities (BOQ) to price a specialist slope-support subcontract. Please prepare it from the supplied scope note, site investigation report, design calculation, and drawings.

Before drafting any line, check what standing procedures are available to you and
follow those whose stated scope matches this work.

Save one Excel workbook at `/workspace/边坡支护专业分包招标清单.xlsx`.

Use the exact three section names in `招标范围及方案版本分段说明.md` as the workbook's main sections. For each line item, show the section, sequence number, item name, item characteristics, measurement rule, unit, quantity, pre-tax unit price, pre-tax total, evidence reference, quantity basis, and basis status. An evidence reference must name a supplied file and locate the supporting page, section, clause, table, axis, note, or drawing detail. Use one of these basis status values: `source-stated`, `calculated`, `provisional`, or `review-required`. Leave the pre-tax unit price blank for bidders. Make the pre-tax total formula-based or otherwise ready to calculate.

Include an audit register in the same workbook. It must contain parameter, uncertainty, and closure records, each with a record ID, applicable construction section, linked BOQ items, source or input, adopted value or issue, check or action, and status. Cover every main section with parameter and closure records, and record material assumptions or source conflicts that require tender review.

Chinese or English headings are acceptable. Keep the technical content suitable for a Chinese tender review.
