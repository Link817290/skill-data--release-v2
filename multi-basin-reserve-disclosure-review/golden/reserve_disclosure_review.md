# Reserve disclosure exception review

## Filing-readiness conclusion

The package is not ready to be reused without correction. The current-period PUD movement conflict, the product-to-BOE classification issue, and the comparative-period category swap require coordinated correction (R-01, R-02, R-03). Their value-table effect must be recalculated rather than inferred from volumes (R-04). Current support is also insufficient to close the population-wide PUD plan and issuer-level applied-technology disclosures (R-05, R-06).

## Matters requiring correction or qualification

### R-01 - The current-year proved-undeveloped extension/discovery line conflicts with its own additions narrative.

The printed bridge closes to 24,128 MBoe, but arithmetic closure does not validate a negative extension/discovery classification. The table and the description cannot both represent the movement as printed; the ending PUD balance is not challenged by this category exception.

Action: Obtain the reserve-engineer movement schedule, correct the PUD category allocation and signs, and conform the adjacent narrative without assuming that the parenthesized amount merely needs its sign reversed.

### R-02 - The current-year product tables reproduce the negative equivalent-unit extension amount, but they do not cure its category meaning.

The product-to-BOE tie-out shows consistent propagation of the printed classification, not a valid negative discovery. The supplied package does not establish the corrected split between extensions and revisions.

Action: Require a property-level reserve movement schedule, correct all product and BOE category layers together, and retain the ending-balance tie-out.

### R-03 - The comparative-year BOE summary moved an organic extension amount into purchases even though the product tables report no purchase.

This is a cross-table category swap. Reclassifying the 1,945 MBoe does not change the comparative-year ending reserve quantity, but it changes the reported acquisition-versus-organic history.

Action: Move 1,945 MBoe in the comparative BOE summary from purchases to extensions and discoveries, then reassess every trend or replacement statement that uses the affected comparative categories.

### R-04 - Quantity-category exceptions require review of the standardized-measure movement categories, but the corrected dollar split cannot be derived here.

The upstream extension/revision classifications are unreliable, so these downstream change categories may also be misstated. Volume alone cannot allocate value because property timing, prices, costs, taxes and discounting are not supplied at the level needed. The $379.400 million ending measure is not disproved merely by a category presentation error.

Action: Have accounting and the reserve engineers rerun or bridge the property-level cash flows and correct the change categories; do not prorate dollars from the volume reclassification.

### R-05 - The package states a PUD policy but does not demonstrate that the complete year-end PUD population is covered by a current adopted development plan.

Those policy statements do not provide booking dates, scheduled conversion dates, plan approval, or exception support for the entire current population, including acquired PUDs. The record supports a disclosure-evidence gap, not a conclusion that any specific PUD is invalid.

Action: Obtain the location-level PUD aging roll-forward, adopted plan and approval evidence, expected conversion dates, delay history, and support for any location extending beyond the ordinary development horizon.

### R-06 - The issuer-level filing lacks a general discussion connecting technologies actually applied to material reserve additions, although evaluator exhibits partially supply method evidence.

A definition, an input list, and management's control description are not a general discussion of applied technology. The independent reports mitigate the omission within their scopes but do not replace management's coherent issuer-level explanation or connect methods to the material additions.

Action: Add an issuer-level discussion of the technologies used to establish reasonable certainty for material additions, linked to the relevant reserve classes and properties while preserving evaluator scope.

### R-07 - Significant purchase movements are not described consistently across the reserve-change narrative and comparative reserve tables.

The current acquisition is directly connected to the reserve addition, while the comparative reserve-change presentation requires the reader to assemble the connection elsewhere. This is a narrative-completeness matter separate from the comparative category swap identified in R-03.

Action: Add or cross-reference a concise explanation of each significant purchase movement by period, geography and transaction, after correcting the comparative classification.

## Cleared high-risk apparent inconsistencies

### R-08 - The independent engineer reports reconcile to the issuer's ending proved reserve total within rounding; this does not clear movement classifications.

The four reports support geographic coverage and the rounded ending quantity. They are independent estimates or evaluations that rely on identified management inputs; they are not an audit of issuer controls and do not validate reserve movement labels.

Disposition: CLEARED WITH SCOPE LIMITATION. Retain the ending-balance tie-out as a clearance, preserve each evaluator's scope and reliance wording, and remediate the category exceptions separately.

### R-09 - The apparent difference between a benchmark price and a property price is supported as an adjustment rather than a reserve-pricing error.

A property-adjusted price need not equal the unadjusted reference price. The stated adjustment basis and constant-price treatment explain the difference, so the figures should not be forced to match.

Disposition: CLEARED. Document the clearance and retain the adjustment support; no price correction is proposed from the supplied evidence.

### R-10 - The external summary places SEC proved measures near a larger 2P working-interest measure, but the figures are not a reconcilable pair on the supplied bases.

Reserve class, framework, ownership, price and cost assumptions differ. A shared discount rate does not make the measures comparable, and the source says a reconciliation is not practicable. The issuer-specific tax definition also overrides a generic assumption that PV-10 excludes tax.

Disposition: CLEARED AS INCOMPARABLE BASES; QUALIFY PRESENTATION. Keep the measures visibly separated, repeat the basis qualifications near each headline figure, and do not present $686.6 million as a bridge to the SEC standardized measure or as fair value.
