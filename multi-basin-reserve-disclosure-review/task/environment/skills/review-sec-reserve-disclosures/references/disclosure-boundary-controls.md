# Disclosure Boundary Controls

Use this reference for matters where a table can be read correctly but the conclusion
depends on regulatory scope, engineer reliance, or measurement basis.

## Undeveloped Reserve Plans

Test the current reserve population, not only the issuer's stated policy. Strong support
normally connects each material undeveloped location to its initial booking date, an
adopted development plan, expected drilling or conversion timing, approval status,
capital allocation, and any supported reason for delay.

A statement that management reviews undeveloped reserves annually is evidence of a
control, not evidence that every current location satisfies the development horizon.
Likewise, a generic rule definition or risk factor does not establish current compliance.

When the supplied package lacks location-level aging or plan evidence, the appropriate
result is a bounded evidence request. Do not assume either compliance or invalidity.

## Applied Technology

Separate these artifacts:

- regulatory definition of reliable technology;
- list of geological and engineering data supplied;
- statement of estimation method;
- explanation of technologies actually used for material additions;
- evidence that the technology performed consistently in the relevant formation or an
  appropriate analogue.

Definitions and data lists are context. Applied-method discussion is the direct evidence.
An engineer exhibit may fill part of the record for properties within its scope, but the
issuer should still present a coherent general discussion when material reserve changes
depend on those methods.

## Engineer Scope and Reliance

Do not infer a stronger assurance level than the report states. Capture the exact verb:
estimated, evaluated, reviewed, or audited. Note any management-supplied interests,
production, prices, costs, schedules, or well data accepted without independent
verification.

Coverage should be recomputed on a comparable basis. Reconcile product units, royalty or
entitlement treatment, and geographic scope before comparing an engineer total with the
issuer's company total. A rounded tie-out can clear the ending quantity while leaving a
movement-classification or disclosure-control issue open.

## Price Differences

Reserve reports commonly show both a reference benchmark and property-adjusted prices.
Differences may reflect quality, transportation, location, marketing, or contractual
terms. Check the stated adjustment basis and whether prices are held constant where the
reporting basis requires it. Do not compare an adjusted property realization directly
with an unadjusted benchmark and call the difference an error.

## Competing Reserve Frameworks

Build a basis card before comparing any two headline figures:

| dimension | examples of possible bases |
| --- | --- |
| certainty class | proved; proved plus probable |
| framework | SEC definitions; resource-management guidance |
| ownership | net entitlement; working interest before royalties |
| economics | prescribed historical average; management escalation |
| value | standardized measure; non-GAAP discounted cash flow |
| taxes | excluded; included; jurisdiction-specific treatment |

The same discount percentage does not make two value measures comparable. A reconciliation
requires a bridge for all material basis differences. If the package expressly defines a
measure differently from its usual convention, follow the package's definition and call
out the departure rather than overwriting it with a textbook assumption.

## Evidence Language

Use conclusion strength that matches the record:

- **Correct** when the supplied documents establish the existing disclosure is wrong and
  support the required change.
- **Qualify** when the package supports a narrower statement than the one presented.
- **Clear** when an apparent inconsistency is explained by scope, units, rounding, or a
  disclosed basis difference.
- **Request evidence** when a decision depends on information not supplied.

These are working-paper functions, not mandatory labels. Explain the evidence and action
in the row so a different but equivalent disposition vocabulary remains reviewable.

## Stop Rules

Stop before converting a missing schedule into a factual conclusion. Common stop points
include unavailable booking dates, property-level cash flows, approval records, evaluator
workpapers, and bridges between incompatible reserve frameworks. State exactly what would
resolve the matter and who should supply it.
