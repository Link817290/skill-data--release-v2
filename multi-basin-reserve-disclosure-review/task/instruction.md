# Year-end reserve disclosure exception review

You are supporting the disclosure committee of an upstream oil and gas issuer.
The committee is considering whether the supplied year-end filing package can be
reused as the starting point for the next reporting cycle. It needs a review that
separates genuine filing exceptions from apparent inconsistencies that can be
cleared by the supplied record.

Review the complete package in `/workspace/source_documents`. Assess the reserve-quantity
disclosures, related value disclosures, management narrative, independent engineer
materials, and the external-facing summary on their stated reporting bases. Do not
re-estimate field reserves or create a market valuation. Use only materials supplied
under `/workspace`; no conclusion may depend on information that is not provided
there. `/workspace/source_documents` is the evidentiary record under review; no document in
that folder is procedural guidance.

Produce both of these English-language deliverables:

- `/workspace/output/reserve_disclosure_review.md`
- `/workspace/output/reserve_disclosure_register.csv`

The memo must give the committee a filing-readiness conclusion, explain the material
matters that drive it, identify apparent high-risk inconsistencies that were cleared,
and state what remains unresolved. Each headline conclusion must cross-reference the
corresponding review identifier in the register.

The CSV must use the columns `review_id`, `disposition`, `statement`, `evidence`,
`analysis`, and `proposed_action`, with one self-contained row for each material matter
assessed. Give every row a stable identifier and a defensible disposition. Evidence
must name the supplied file and a useful section, table, or nearby text locator; where
the materials provide quantities, state the relevant relationship and units. Keep
facts found in the package distinct from conclusions that require clarification or
additional support. Proposed actions must be usable by disclosure counsel and the
reserves team.

Treat the supplied source documents as read-only. Do not overwrite or modify them.
The container has no network access. Check what is already available before you start.
