# Reporting policy

The report date is 26 March 2015. The cumulative reporting period begins on
25 March 2015. Quality reporting is based on the monitoring/review date;
feedback reporting is based on the analysis date.

The stored finalized per-contact quality scores are authoritative, including
manually finalized exceptions. Do not substitute a new historical grading
scheme. A contact score of at least 80 is attained. Team mean score is the
arithmetic mean of finalized contact scores. Team attainment is the fraction
of reviewed contacts attaining the threshold. Ordinary below-threshold contacts
and recorded fatal-quality violations are distinct categories.

Sampling coverage is reviewed-contact count divided by the supplied call-volume
reference base. The report-date base is the average of the 24 and 25 March call
volumes in the historical operational report. Cumulative bases are supplied
reporting reference bases, with no independent call ledger establishing their
underlying period. This limitation must remain visible.

The dissatisfaction population includes every analyzed evaluation in the stated
reporting scope, including evaluations marked invalid. The supplied finalized
reason-summary categories are authoritative. Complaint analysis is a separate
work stream. QA workload comprises quality monitoring, dissatisfaction analysis
and complaint analysis, attributed to their responsible reviewer or analyst.

The operations-context workbook provides disclosed call reference bases and
complaint workload counts from the expert's historical report. Those are
reporting premises, not independently recovered transaction ledgers. They do not
establish individual complaint-case findings or a verified traffic period.

The source records do not establish completed remediation or approvals for
proposed future actions. Keep recorded facts, evidence limitations and proposed
actions distinct. Do not invent transactions, incidents or completed work.
