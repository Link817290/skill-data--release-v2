# Shutdown Maintenance Rubric

## Authority and Measurement

The executable authority is `task/tests/test.sh` and its referenced scoring
parameters. Deterministic checks own facts, dates, arithmetic and cross-sheet
consistency. The source-bound mixed judge owns professional equivalence, authority,
handoff and conditional planning language. Missing judge services, invalid
responses and replay misses are infrastructure errors, not candidate zeroes.

Seven deterministic owners measure package coverage, duration/window feasibility,
resource capacity, coupled precedence, documented lexicographic priorities,
follow-up dates and table/Gantt agreement. The public planning conditions specify
the ordered priorities. Exact reference starts are not required. Enumerated
feasibility establishes the current bound under the provided estimates only.

The content judge measures eleven source-bound owners: scope grain and
area identity; readiness and paired deferral; estimated-duration status; ordered
business priorities; a feasible and consistent T milestone; execution controls and
closeout; candidate handback and electrical boundaries; actual-state/change
control; continuous standby and response; the report trigger; and the oil
follow-up trigger and scope. Each owner uses its declared completion scale. The
judge distinguishes a candidate milestone from authorization or an
observed result and does not infer controls from a coincidentally correct date.
It must not double-penalize a calendar error merely repeated in a note. No visual
style score or hidden staffing preference applies.
Shared workbook conditions and cross-sheet evidence apply to their corresponding
work packages and milestones; the same condition need not be repeated in each
row. Prospective candidate dates with an actual-event update rule are accepted.
Execution checks use the source's approved-plan and permission boundary, without
adding a blanket test-readiness statement before every construction activity.

The Bench strict score uses declared additive responsibility domains. The
source-bound business completion score receives 90%; its schedule, readiness,
duration-basis, execution, handback, state, standby, report and oil-analysis
controls retain local weighted partial credit. An embedded independent validation
receipt receives 5%, and a maintainable event-update mechanism receives 5%.
The final formula is
`0.90 * business_completion + 0.05 * validation + 0.05 * update`. The
validation receipt accepts equivalent tables, formulas, calculations or recorded
check results and never requires a specific script or written proof. The update
owner accepts formulas or a structured manual workflow and does not require
macros or invented actual results. Trajectory/process review remains separate and
does not enter this formula.

The lenient score is the conjunction of five independently measured must-haves:
a feasible core schedule, a calendar matching that schedule, a provisional-
duration basis, a safe candidate handback boundary, and continuous standby
handoff. Strict and lenient rewards are both emitted from the same measured
receipts.

Execution review also checks whether package-level dates are improperly treated
as independently energizable assets. Missing electrical associations must remain
an execution condition, without inventing dependencies absent from the inputs.

## Weight Rationale

The 90% business component is dominant because schedule, feasibility, readiness,
handback and follow-up are the substantive maintenance deliverable. The 5%
validation receipt rewards an independently inspectable check without requiring a
particular script. The 5% update mechanism rewards revising affected milestones
after an actual event, without rewarding invented completed results. This preserves
local partial credit instead of letting a small documentation omission erase a
usable plan.
