description: Preserve maintenance scope, coupled work, handback conditions and post-restoration obligations when preparing a site execution programme.
---

Use [scope and coupling](references/scope-and-coupling.md) when bill-of-work items
overlap or are conditional. Use [handover closure](references/handover.md) to attach
service restoration and follow-up to evidence rather than planned dates alone.

`scripts/month_due.py` calculates a calendar-month follow-up date; confirm the
contract's end-of-month convention before using it. Retain actual execution fields
as pending where no result exists.
