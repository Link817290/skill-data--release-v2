# Rubric

Calibrated task-specific Program Judge for the frozen 25-rollout cohort.
Strict is a weighted sum of nine direct owners and three source-supported local chains. Every owner retains direct partial credit; there is no global gamma, power, or damage multiplier. Lenient is an independent binary lens: it is 1.0 only when measurement boundaries P02 are at least half complete, channel actuals and period reconciliation P06 are at least half complete, the five-channel forecast and total reconciliation P10 are complete, and D08 is not triggered. Otherwise lenient is 0.0.
The reference forecast intervals are scenarios, not uniquely required numeric answers.

## Weight and Chain Rationale

The weights follow the business decision structure in the supplied disclosures. P02
has the largest direct accounting weight because basis, sign and period boundaries
determine whether any later comparison is meaningful. P10 is the next largest
owner because a five-channel forecast must reconcile both channel intervals and the
company total; a plausible isolated channel number is not a usable outlook. P12
and P13 cover risk response and regulatory minima, but they are narrower supporting
claims and therefore carry smaller direct weights. P01, P03-P06 and P12-P13 retain
positive partial credit so a report can show correct work even when one source
boundary or calculation is incomplete.

C01 and C02 are local minima rather than global multipliers. C01 protects the
core outlook chain: measurement boundaries, actual channel reconciliation and the
forecast must all be usable. C02 protects the narrower signed-premium forecast
chain. C03 gives most of its completion to the company-specific current and forecast
solvency analysis because that evidence establishes actual headroom; the smaller
threshold-context share keeps the governing 50%/100% minima visible without allowing
their omission to erase otherwise correct capital analysis. A failed chain does not
erase unrelated owners and no chain is a score cap.

D08 and D09 are separate because they describe different source errors. D08
collapses unlike annual-report sales-method shares and signed-premium channel
shares into one trend; D09 uses scale-premium growth as the signed-premium
forecast base. Each is charged once per adopted causal error, while a conditional
warning or an unresolved source discrepancy remains eligible for partial credit.
The reference forecast intervals are scenarios supported by the materials, not
unique numeric targets.
