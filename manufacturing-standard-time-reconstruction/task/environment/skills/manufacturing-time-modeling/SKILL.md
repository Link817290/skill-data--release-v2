# Manufacturing time modeling

Preserve the existing workbook's business structure. Establish station and
element identities before linking records across tables.

Keep the represented quantity and time basis with every measurement. Separate
observation quality, pace assessment, allowances and output normalization rather
than treating all durations as interchangeable.

Connect production indicators to the appropriate measurement basis. Distinguish
theoretical estimates from operational resource decisions, and apply only the
case conventions actually supplied by the task.

This is a reusable way to organize the model, without task formulas, field
solutions or planning answers. Use `spreadsheet-behavior-checks` for generic
inspection and recalculation.
