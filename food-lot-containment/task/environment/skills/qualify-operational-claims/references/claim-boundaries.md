# Claim boundaries

Announcements identify a scope and recommended response. They usually do not establish current stock, the full transaction population, ingredient mass balance or completed customer notification. Known business links are weaker than traceable manufacturing-lot links.

Separate three questions: which products meet the stated identification rule; whether the organization actually holds or distributed them; and what action has been completed. Each requires different evidence. Never use absence of a transaction file as evidence of zero transactions.

Statements such as no illnesses reported, product past date, or no longer expected to be on shelves do not prove absence of contamination or frozen holdings. Preserve the time and entity scope of a negative statement.

For historical tasks, constrain the conclusion to the supplied evidence and requested cutoff. Do not import subsequent closure status or silently claim global completeness. Where the supplied collection covers only selected notices, state that limit without turning the report into a provenance essay.
