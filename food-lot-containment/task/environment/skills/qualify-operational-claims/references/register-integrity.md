# Register integrity at handover

Choose a key at the grain of each table. A product can legitimately appear once per customer in a relationship table; a named summary metric normally has one adopted value. Check repeated summary keys before converting rows into a dictionary. Last-row-wins conversion can hide a conflicting earlier value. Resolve the conflict or retain explicitly distinguished scopes rather than silently replacing one value.

Verify formula cells in both formula and evaluated-value views. A formula with no stored result is not evidence that the business quantity is zero or incorrect. Recalculate in a compatible spreadsheet application, export a copy, and check the evaluated result. When recalculation cannot be performed, disclose that the value remains unverified.

Keep these checks separate from source accuracy. A summary can reconcile to its own register while that register is incomplete; conversely a correct register can have an inconsistent summary. Report each actual mechanism at its own level and avoid multiplying one record error into unrelated findings.
