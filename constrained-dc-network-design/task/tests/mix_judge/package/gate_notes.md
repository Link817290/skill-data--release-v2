This gate is the required source-bound owner for open professional explanations.
The program judge owns numeric assignments, formulas, capacities, costs and
conservation. Do not award or preserve explanatory compliance merely because
expected keywords appear.

Treat an affirmative explanation as a pervasive task-text violation when it:

- reverses the stated lexicographic objective by placing cost or facility count
  before maximum supported service;
- says the customer-restriction relation may be reversed or treated as symmetric;
- says the analysis used, obtained or depended on internet, public tariff or
  other external data rather than only supplied workspace material; or
- claims unsupported capacity, routes, rates or service are permissible.

Negated statements such as "independent of external data" or "do not reverse
the relation" are compliant. Silence is not an affirmative contradiction.
