# Evidence ledger

Create one row for every proposition that may enter a filing:

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Proposition | The complete factual or legal statement |
| Source | File, page or message location |
| Speaker | Contracting party, notice sender, respondent, or neutral record |
| Status | Agreed term, observed event, allegation, denial, request, or inference |
| Time | Event date and document date separately |
| Allowed use | Pleaded fact, party contention, evidence purpose, or unresolved field |

Do not collapse the notice sender's allegations into admitted facts. A rebuttal establishes that the allegation is disputed, while the underlying event still needs its own support. Keep the contract date, performance term, operating territory, payment structure, termination clause, and agreed breach amount attached to the contract rather than to later correspondence.

When two sources differ, state the narrow proposition both support and preserve the difference in the ledger. Use the most specific source locator available and retain the source's actor and polarity.
