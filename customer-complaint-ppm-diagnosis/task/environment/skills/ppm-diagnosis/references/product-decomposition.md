# Product decomposition

For each product, aggregate its observed complaints and its own sales over the
adopted period. Product PPM is the ratio of those totals times one million.
Missing product sales cannot be replaced by overall sales unless the source
explicitly defines a contribution-to-overall metric instead of a product rate.

Complaint share is product complaints divided by the complaints of the declared
comparison population. A product's relative rate is its PPM divided by overall
PPM for the same period. When overall PPM is zero or unavailable, the relative
rate is undefined and must not be fabricated.

Verify that product groups are disjoint and exhaustive before expecting shares
to sum to one. A residual may indicate an uncategorized population rather than
an arithmetic error. Keep that residual visible instead of rescaling shares.

Rank rate and absolute complaint burden separately. A small product with a high
rate and a large product with many complaints raise different priorities. Without
severity or customer-impact evidence, neither ranking is a complete risk score.
