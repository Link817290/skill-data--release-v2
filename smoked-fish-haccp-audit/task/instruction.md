# HACCP plan authorization review

The quality assurance lead must decide whether the proposed HACCP plan excerpt in
`/workspace/review_dossier.pdf` is ready to authorize for a refrigerated,
vacuum-packaged, ready-to-eat hot-smoked fish line. Review its technical adequacy and
implementation readiness against the material supplied in `/workspace`.

Both supplied PDFs contain embedded searchable text. Image rendering is unavailable and
must not be used; review the documents through their embedded text.

Write the result in English as valid JSON at `/workspace/haccp_audit.json`. Include a
clear overall disposition and discrete findings; for every finding state the affected
HACCP element, decision significance, evidence-supported basis, evidence boundary, and
specific corrective recommendation, with PDF page locations that let the quality lead
verify the conclusion.

Treat all supplied files as read-only, do not use the network, and do not rely on any
information that is not provided under `/workspace`.

Review the relevant HACCP and seafood process-control runtime skills available in the
environment before examining the documents.
