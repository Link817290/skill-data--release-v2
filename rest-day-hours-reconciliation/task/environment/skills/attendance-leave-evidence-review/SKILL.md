# Attendance and Leave Evidence Review

Use this method for a multi-record HR review. Read `references/event-evidence.md` before classifying events and `references/closure-and-quality.md` before finalizing findings.

## Establish the record model

Inventory every source, its author or system, its effective period and the meaning of its status fields. Preserve the original files. Where documents need extraction, keep a source-to-extract mapping and verify that dates, identifiers and tables survived.

Build a working event index using stable identifiers. Where a record has no event identifier, join by employee identifier and date, then verify the context. An employee can have several events or activities on the same date; names and dates alone are not a sufficient universal join key.

## Reconstruct chronology

For each event, distinguish the original schedule, a proposed change, approval, publication, notification and the actual activity. Compare timestamps against the shift and leave periods. A system's latest value may describe its current state without proving what the employee knew before the event.

Keep schedule status, employer instruction, attendance, actual work, leave approval, actual rest and payroll treatment as separate observations. For each observation write the evidence that establishes it and the evidence that limits it. Do not turn absence of one record into proof that the underlying event did not occur.

## Resolve and explain

Apply the bundled policy to the evidence, not to the system's completion label. Test whether the relevant prerequisites hold together. Compare contradictory records within the same event and preserve unresolved conflicts. State a conclusion only as strongly as the evidence supports.

Calculate time intervals from the source timestamps and keep the arithmetic inspectable. Distinguish elapsed presence from compensable working time; breaks, non-work activities and missing records can change that relationship. Record units and rounding explicitly.

For every unresolved matter, identify the exact missing fact, the record or responsible person that can establish it, the owner who will obtain it and the business milestone before which the decision must be reviewed. Avoid invented policies, payment amounts or confirmed legal liability.

## Produce and verify the deliverables

Write event findings with local evidence references, then build the calculation and follow-up tables. Use the text report to explain material exceptions and their downstream treatment. Keep identities and decisions consistent across all sheets and the report.

Before saving, reconcile event coverage against the source index; verify every cited location; recalculate intervals; check repeated event presentations for conflicting conclusions; distinguish registered leave from completed leave; and check that each open item has an owner and review milestone. This is a method checklist, not evidence that those checks have actually passed.
