# Specifics the record does not fix, and marked placeholders

Enforcement filings demand more particulars than any adjudication was ever going to record. A party's
current registered address and current representative, the leads on an obligor's assets, an amount
that depends on a published rate, the day a running amount is counted to, the identifying particulars
of a person who is not your client: the genre requires the slot, and the decisions in front of you
often say nothing about it.

Two opposite failures follow, and a draft usually contains both at once. One is invention: a plausible
value appears, and because it is plausible nobody notices it is fabricated until the other side does.
The other is abdication: everything that took any work is marked open, the document turns into a form
with the answers left out, and the work the filing was supposed to do has not been done.

The discipline that avoids both is not a rule about placeholders. It is a classification, done field by
field, before drafting.

## 1. Classify every specific into one of three classes

Take the genre's field list, that is, the party block fields, the identification of the object, every
figure, every date and the body addressed, and put each field in exactly one class.

**Class A: fixed by the record, or derivable from it.** The record states it, or it follows from
something the record states by arithmetic you can carry out or by a rule you can state. Anything the
instruction supplies as a fact is in this class, and so is everything downstream of it.

**Class B: required by the genre, open on this record.** The document cannot omit the slot, and the
record genuinely does not fix its value, and no rule closes the gap. A current registered address that
no document in the bundle records. An amount that turns on a published rate series the record does not
contain. Identifying particulars of a party the decisions refer to only by name.

**Class C: not required at all.** A fact that would help if it were true, that nothing establishes: an
allegation of back-dating, of collusion, of a defect in a document, of an asset held somewhere. Also
here: an item the enforcement title never awarded, dressed up as though it had.

Doing this as a written list, before drafting, is the whole technique. Its value is that it forces
Class B to be small, because most of what a first reading calls "missing" turns out to be Class A once
you look for the rule that closes it.

## 2. Class A: write the value, and show the derivation where there is one

- A value the record states is written as the record states it.
- A value you derived is written with its derivation beside it, in the text, in one clause: the inputs
  it came from and the operation. A sum of three figures shows the three figures. A date derived from
  an event and a period shows the event and the period. A base that is the total of the money
  obligations shows what was included.
- Where two documents give different values for the same field, the later document governs, and the
  filing states only the value it uses.
- **Never place a marked placeholder here.** A field you could have closed and marked open instead is
  the more damaging of the two errors in a filing that is meant to be acted on, because it moves work
  from you to the reader and invites the filing to be sent back.

Watch for the fields that look open and are not. The day a period expires and the day the consequences
of non-performance start running are derived, not reported. Which body receives the filing is derived.
Which persons may be made subject to enforcement is derived. The base of a derived sum is derived. The
segmentation of a formula is transcribed. None of these is a candidate for a placeholder, and marking
any of them open concedes the judgement the filing exists to make.

## 3. Class B: keep the slot, keep everything fixed around it, mark only what is open

This is the move that separates a usable filing from a form.

**Mark the narrowest part.** Do not mark a field when only a component of it is open, and do not mark
a claim when only its final number is open. A running money item whose rate series you cannot look up
still has a base, a starting date, an end expressed as running to satisfaction, and a named rate
standard; all four are written out, and the provisional total alone is marked. What you deliver is
then a complete, checkable claim with one value left to be computed, not a gap.

**Keep the reasoning, mark the value.** The purpose of the marked placeholder is to identify a value
that has to be supplied; it is never a substitute for an analysis. If the reason a value is open is
that you have not decided something, that is Class A work that has not been done.

**Never carry a placeholder into arithmetic.** A total computed from an open base is not a total. If a
component is open, everything downstream of it is open too, and the downstream item is marked as well
rather than being given a number that looks determined. A provisional grand total that silently
contains an unresolved component is worse than no total.

**One convention, used identically.** Pick a single visible marker and use it for every Class B field
in the document. It has to be impossible to mistake for a value, findable by a search of the text, and
it has to say what belongs in the slot rather than that something is absent. A marker that names the
missing item is an instruction to whoever completes the filing; a bare blank is a defect.

**Do not explain it.** No note beside it, no sentence about what the materials did not contain, no
apology. The marker carries all the explanation the document is allowed.

**Count them at the end.** A document whose party block is mostly markers, or whose operative part
contains more markers than values, has not been drafted. Go back: most of those fields are Class A.

## 4. Class C: do not assert, and do not mark either

A marked placeholder is not a licence to assert. Putting a marker next to an allegation nothing
supports does not make the allegation safe; it produces a document that makes a serious accusation and
admits in the same line that it has no basis for it.

Three treatments, in order of preference:

1. **Leave it out.** Most Class C material is the draft reaching for an argument it does not need.
2. **State what the record does support**, narrowly. Where a document's absence from a register or a
   file is itself recorded, that absence is a Class A fact and can be stated as such. What may not be
   stated is the inference you would like to draw from it.
3. **State it in its weakened form, once.** Where the point genuinely matters and the record neither
   establishes nor excludes it, it may be put as something the record does not exclude, in that form,
   in one sentence, and never repeated as though it had been established. Do not build a later
   paragraph on it and do not rely on it in the operative part.

An item the title never awarded belongs to Class C in one respect only: it may not be presented as
awarded. Whether it may be claimed at all as an extension is decided by the route reference.

## 5. Where the instruction requires marked placeholders

Where the instruction directs that facts the materials do not establish appear as clearly marked
placeholders, that direction governs Class B and nothing else. It does not authorise Class C
assertions, it does not excuse Class A work, and it does not license a document composed of markers.
Read it as: this filing may be delivered with named gaps in it rather than with invented values, and
the gaps must be visible.

## 6. The party block in particular

The party block is where Class B concentrates, and where invention is easiest and least defensible.

- Names and registration identifiers: search every document in the inputs before concluding a field is
  open; party blocks of decisions, headers, signature blocks and service records each carry different
  fields.
- The person who represents an entity, and that person's office: take the later document. Where the
  later document gives the person but not the full office, write what it gives and do not complete the
  title from the earlier one or from convention.
- An address no document records: Class B. Keep the field, mark it.
- Identifying particulars of an individual who is not your client: take what the enforcement file
  records and no more.

## 7. Self-check

1. Every field in the genre's list sits in exactly one class, and you can say which.
2. No date, no forum, no person subject to enforcement and no derived base is marked open.
3. Every marked slot is the narrowest part that is genuinely open, and everything fixed around it is
   written out.
4. No marker appears inside a figure that is then used in a calculation, and nothing downstream of a
   marked value is presented as determined.
5. One marker convention, used identically, findable, and naming what belongs there.
6. No sentence anywhere explains a marker or describes what the inputs failed to contain.
7. Nothing the record does not establish is asserted; the one weakened form, if used, appears once and
   nothing rests on it.
