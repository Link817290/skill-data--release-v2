# Filings in the enforcement of a decision that has taken effect

Everything in this family sits downstream of an adjudication that is over. You are not proving the
claim; the claim has been decided, and what is left is to turn a decided claim into acts of
enforcement, or to defend the reach of those acts against someone who says they cannot touch a
particular asset. That single fact drives the whole method.

- **An instrument already fixes what may be done, and to whom.** Call it the enforcement title. It is
  the operative part of the decision that took effect, or the ruling that a court made inside the
  enforcement case. The title is a ceiling, not a starting point: what it did not impose cannot be
  applied for, and whoever it did not bind cannot be made subject to enforcement.
- **The reader acts rather than decides.** An enforcement officer has to be able to carry out what
  you write without asking a question, and a court deciding an enforcement action has to see at a
  glance which side of the enforcement case each party stands on. Both consequences are about
  definiteness, not persuasion.
- **The filing has to be in time, and the time runs from a procedural event.** Dates in this family
  are derived, not reported. One event starts them, one convention counts them, and everything
  downstream has to reconcile with that choice.
- **The record will not fix every specific the genre requires.** This family asks for fields the
  decisions were never going to contain. Handling them is a discipline, not an improvisation:
  `references/unsupported-specifics-and-placeholders.md`.

## Before drafting: read every reference

Open and read every reference listed in "Reference routing" at the end of this file before you write
the first line of the draft, whatever the deliverable is. Then apply only the parts whose conditions
match your task. Reading is unconditional; applying is conditional.

## Step 1: fix the coordinates of the enforcement

In your working notes, before anything else, record: which decisions are in the inputs and which of
them took effect; what each one's operative part imposes; which body made each; whether an
enforcement case has already been opened and, if so, what has been done in it and what has been ruled;
and what the client is asking you to achieve. Name the procedural posture in one sentence: enforcement
is being started, or enforcement has met an obstacle that a ruling has now created.

Then fix, as a separate line in the notes, which body this filing is addressed to. It is fixed by the
enforcement procedure and is not a choice:
`references/enforcement-title-and-reach.md` section 5.

Verification: you can name the enforcement title, and you can say in one sentence why the body you
have named is the one that receives this filing rather than another body that also appears in the
inputs.

Counterexample: where several decisions are in the inputs, the one that decides the duty and the one
that makes it final are often different documents. Do not collapse them into whichever is later;
record both and what each contributes.

## Step 2: fix the reach of the enforcement title, on both sides

The reach has a persons side and a content side, and they are worked separately. Apply
`references/enforcement-title-and-reach.md` sections 1 to 3.

- **Persons.** Only someone on whom the operative part lays a duty can be made subject to enforcement.
  Being a party, being discussed in the reasons, or being the target of a claim that was dismissed is
  not enough, and a decision that leaves the decision below standing does not widen the persons side.
- **Content.** Go through the operative part limb by limb and write each limb down as its own row. A
  limb that was refused, a limb the decision left to a later determination, and a grievance the
  decision never addressed are not rows.

Anything the client wants that is not a row is an extension. Keep extensions in a separate part of
the notes. They may still go into the filing, but never as something the title awarded.

Verification: for every person you will name as subject to enforcement, and for every item you will
ask for, you can point to the limb of the operative part it comes from; the extensions list is
written out, even if it is empty.

## Step 3: fix the party block in enforcement designations

Parties in this family carry the designation the enforcement case gives them, not the one the
concluded action gave them. Where the deliverable is a pleading in an action that arises out of the
enforcement, each party carries two labels at once. The rule, and the fields each party line holds,
are in `references/enforcement-filing-baseline.md` sections 2 and 3.

Where the inputs record the same party differently in different documents, take the later record and
say nothing about the earlier one. Where a field has no source at all, Step 5 decides what happens to
it.

Verification: every party line carries a designation that belongs to the enforcement case; no party
appears under two different designations anywhere in the document.

## Step 4: build the date chain

One event starts it, and every other date in the filing is derived from that event by a stated rule.
Build the whole chain in the notes before you write any of it into the draft, and carry one baseline
through all of it. Apply `references/enforcement-title-and-reach.md` section 4.

Verification: every date in the draft is either the starting event itself or is reachable from it by
a rule you can state; the date the filing carries falls inside the window it has to fall inside; no
two dates in the document imply different baselines.

## Step 5: classify every specific the document needs

Before drafting, go field by field through what the genre requires and put each one in exactly one of
three classes: fixed by the record or derivable from it; required by the genre but genuinely open on
this record; or not required at all. Each class has one correct treatment, and the failure that
matters here is applying the wrong one. Apply
`references/unsupported-specifics-and-placeholders.md` in full.

Verification: you have a written list with each field in one class; nothing is in two; and for every
field in the second class you can say which narrowest part of it is open and what remains fixed
around it.

## Step 6: build the operative part, then write

Route on what the deliverable is. The two routes do not mix, and a document that carries parts of
both has gone wrong.

- **An application to the enforcement organ**, asking it to act against an obligor. There is no
  contested reasoning in it. Apply `references/enforcement-application.md`.
- **A pleading in an action that a ruling on an objection opened**, contested, addressed to a court.
  Apply `references/objection-action-pleading.md`.

Whichever route applies, the frame around the body, the designations, the authority citations and the
length are in `references/enforcement-filing-baseline.md`.

Verification: every operative item names a person, an act and an object or a sum, in a form an officer
could carry out; nothing in the operative part depends on a fact stated nowhere in the document.

## Self-check before delivering

1. The body addressed in the header and at the sign-off is the one the enforcement procedure fixes,
   and no other body is named anywhere as the recipient.
2. Every person named as subject to enforcement carries a duty under the operative part of the title;
   no person whose claim or whose liability the title refused appears there.
3. Every operative item traces to a limb of the title, or is identified in your notes as an extension
   and is not presented in the document as something the title awarded.
4. Every asset is identified by what makes it unique, and every sum by either a figure or a base, a
   period and a rate standard; an officer reading the operative part alone could act on each item.
5. The date chain reconciles: one starting event, one counting convention, one baseline, and the
   filing's own date falls inside the window that applies to it.
6. Every specific in the document sits in the class Step 5 put it in, and carries that class's
   treatment; no placeholder stands in for a judgement you were asked to make, and no placeholder
   has been carried into arithmetic.
7. Nothing from the list in `references/enforcement-filing-baseline.md` section 5 is present: no
   drafting notes, no concession against your client, no account of what the inputs failed to
   contain.
8. Every rule relied on is named with the instrument that carries it, beside the proposition it
   decides, and no article number has been invented.
9. The designations are consistent everywhere they appear, including the sign-off.
10. You read all five references before drafting.

## Failure modes

- **Treating the title as a starting point.** Re-arguing the merits, widening a sum, or adding an
  obligor the title did not bind. The decided case is the ceiling; an application that exceeds it is
  refused at the counter, and a pleading that re-opens it is answered in one line.
- **Taking the persons side from the party list.** Everyone who appeared gets copied across, including
  those the decision let go. This is the single most common way the filing fails, because it looks
  like careful transcription.
- **Naming the body that decided last.** The body that receives the filing is fixed by the enforcement
  procedure, and it is frequently not the one whose decision made the duty final.
- **Reporting dates instead of deriving them.** Copying a date out of the inputs and treating the
  chain as done, so the filing never establishes that it is in time or that the obligation is already
  overdue.
- **Mixing the two routes.** A reasoned argument inside an application to the enforcement organ, or a
  schedule of sums to be collected inside a pleading about whether an asset may be reached at all.
- **Inventing the fields the record does not hold**, or, in reaction to that, marking as open
  everything that took any work to establish. Both are failures of the same step, in opposite
  directions.
- **Arithmetic on an open figure.** A total produced from a base that is itself unresolved reads as a
  determined amount and is not one.
- **Asserting a defect nothing shows.** Saying that a document was back-dated, never registered or
  arranged collusively, where the record records nothing either way, destroys the paragraph it sits in.
