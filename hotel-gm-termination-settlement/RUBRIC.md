## Weight rationale

The 85/10/5 split follows the downstream settlement risk. Core results dominate
because an incorrect adopted amount or a failure to propagate an upstream base changes
what HR, legal, or finance would accrue or pay. Judgment and status carry 10 points
because confusing confirmed, conditional, approved, pending, and paid states can still
cause an incorrect action even when an isolated amount is arithmetically right.
Reviewability carries 5 points because it enables independent approval but cannot
compensate for a wrong liability conclusion.

Within the core results, annual leave and non-compete each carry 18 points because
both require a multi-step base, period, and status chain and both propagate into other
settlement views. Historical performance carries 15 because its supported range feeds
annual leave, non-compete, and totals. Cross-item totals carry 17 because they are the
final finance control over those upstream decisions. Smaller self-contained items have
lower weights because an error remains local. These are responsibility and propagation
weights; they reflect the relative business materiality of the underlying obligations.

## 4. Strict scoring rules

1. **Only the adopted value counts.** What was adopted is read from the main results table, the payment recommendation, the adoption markers and the parameters the totals actually use. A correct figure appearing only as a candidate, a risk note, a pending item or an unadopted scenario earns nothing for that result.
2. **A conflicting headline conclusion means incomplete.** If one item carries two mutually exclusive "should adopt" conclusions without stating the applicable condition, the selection rule and the corresponding total, that item scores 0.
3. **"Pending confirmation" is not a disclaimer.** Putting a wrong parameter into the main sheet or the total and then labelling it pending is still scored as the adopted wrong value.
4. **An upstream error is not rescued by a downstream coincidence.** Reaching the same amount through a wrong policy parameter, period, unit or status earns nothing for the affected results.
5. **Propagation must be completed.** If the historical performance shortfall is missing or the range is wrong, that item scores 0, and so do the annual-leave base propagation, the non-compete base propagation and the corresponding high and low ends of the total. These are distinct deliverables, not double counting.
6. **Evidence and formatting do not compensate for wrong answers.** Citing materials, adding worksheets, using formulas, polishing the layout or listing many pending items does not raise the score of a wrong core conclusion.
7. **Reasonable equivalent expressions earn credit.** No fixed sheet name, field order or wording is required, but the meaning, adoption status, parameters and figures must map unambiguously.
8. **Numeric tolerance.** Final amounts accept only per-item rounding differences within 0.10. The historical performance range must stay 3,800–11,400; tolerance is not a substitute for it.

## 6. Automatic zero

Any name, ID number, mobile number, detailed address, bank account or un-redacted internal
voucher scores 0 for the whole task.

## 7. Semantic and consistency boundaries

- Adopted settlement conclusions, status distinctions and policy judgements are
  evaluated by an evidence-bound Responses judge using candidate quotations and the
  supplied source packet. Equivalent cross-row and formula-linked expressions pass;
  merely mentioning a correct number does not. Deterministic code checks workbook
  structure, amounts, formulas, source coordinates and privacy only.
- Independent professional sub-results retain independent partial credit. In
  particular, the severance calculation is separate from its final conditional
  classification, while annual-leave days/tier, adopted amount and wage-base
  propagation are separate owners. An error in one does not erase a completed other
  owner, but no owner receives credit from a number that was merely mentioned.
- Workbook formula replay supports common arithmetic, aggregate, rounding,
  comparison, Boolean and conditional formulas. An unsupported formula is reported
  as coverage unavailable for that formula; it is not itself a proven inconsistency.
  A proven inconsistency requires a successfully replayed formula/cache mismatch or
  a source-derived parameter/roll-up contradiction.
- Lenient completion is independent of strict points. It requires every settlement
  owner to have a source-bound adopted conclusion and its required deterministic
  amount or calculation chain, sufficient source/formula evidence, and semantic
  separation of confirmed, conditional, unknown and continuing obligations.
