# Termination settlement audit

Produce a settlement result that an HR, legal or finance reviewer can recompute independently
from the original materials. Every item must carry four things: its disposition, the amount or
an explicit statement of the uncertainty, the calculation logic, and where the evidence sits.

No web search or external pages at any point. Work only from the task materials and the
reference files shipped with this skill; where the basis is insufficient, list the item as
needing further evidence rather than filling the gap from outside.

For any substantive settlement task, read
[references/execution-manual.md](references/execution-manual.md) in full before you identify
items or compute amounts. The manual carries the item-generation sweep, the evidence rules,
the decision checks, the calculation method and the delivery structure.

## Order of work

1. Inventory every input file and build a dated index of the materials; record evidence that is unreadable, missing, duplicated, superseded or internally contradictory.
2. Generate the candidate list from the materials themselves. Do not rely on the request to name each entitlement or obligation for you.
3. Before computing anything, build a shared parameter ledger that keeps raw facts, adopted values, formula results and judgement calls apart.
4. For disputed facts, build an evidence matrix and state why you adopted what you did. A signature adds evidential weight but does not automatically override a mandatory rule, a scope of application, a validity period or what was actually performed.
5. Classify every candidate as payable, not payable, already settled, conditionally payable, or pending evidence. Do not substitute zero for uncertainty — and do not use "pending" as a way of avoiding a call you can already make. Pending is for items where the materials genuinely cannot settle the amount. Where the chain from rule to parameter to figure is complete, adopt the figure, even if you can imagine a further document that would corroborate it. An item parked as pending contributes nothing to the settlement; an adopted figure with its uncertainty noted alongside is what the reviewer needs.
6. Compute only once the period, inclusion scope, unit, cap, multiplier and amounts already paid are settled.
7. Deliver the parameter table, the per-item settlement table, the evidence index and the open questions, then run the reverse final check from the manual.

## Standing requirements

- Keep three things apart: facts from the materials, the professional basis shipped with this skill, and your own judgement on this case.
- Never quietly patch a suspect material. Record the contradiction and what it does to the result.
- Each amount belongs to exactly one item, and each conclusion maps to specific evidence. A keyword appearing nearby is not an evidence chain.
- Distinguish clearly between a monthly standard, the amount payable as at the settlement date, and the lump sum.
- Where the evidence is insufficient, keep the uncertainty and say what material would resolve it.
- Do not disclose personal or internal identifiers that the settlement judgement does not need.
