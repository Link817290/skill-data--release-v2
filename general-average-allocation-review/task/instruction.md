# General-average allocation review

The marine claims team is reassessing a completed general-average adjustment after
a later final judgment changed the collection posture. Review the supplied records
and prepare an English technical memorandum at
`/workspace/deliverables/general_average_review.md` together with an English
reconciliation schedule at
`/workspace/deliverables/general_average_schedule.csv`. The deliverables must cover
the adjustment arithmetic; the basis and treatment of the ship, cargo including
freight, containers, and bunkers; and the post-judgment collection position. The
schedule must also provide an auditable roll-forward from the gross section totals
through amounts outside general average, admitted general average, commission,
rule-dependent interest, adjustment fees, and the final funds. The memorandum must
address adjusters' notes that materially change classification and the disposition of
each affected account or security population, without transcribing the full voucher
population. State the supported account actions and the evidential limits of statements
about payment, security, cash administration, and collection. Tie material conclusions
to pinpoint locations in the supplied records. Reconcile materially different causal or
legal characterizations and report the authoritative effect of the complete decision
record. State unresolved matters rather than assuming them.

Use only the materials supplied under `/workspace`; no conclusion may depend on
information not provided there. Network access is unavailable, and the supplied
materials are read-only.

Check what is already available before you start.
