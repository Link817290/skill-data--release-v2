# Investment Efficiency

Investment efficiency is `revenue / fixed assets`. Use consolidated revenue and the
**ending** fixed-asset balance in consistent units for report-methodology
reproduction; run a period-average denominator only as a separately labeled
sensitivity, never mixed into the primary reading. Do not fabricate an average
denominator when the opening fixed-asset balance is unavailable — a missing opening
balance is a review condition, not a number to invent.

## Construction-in-progress distortion

Fixed assets exclude construction-in-progress (CIP): assets still being built are
not yet productive. When CIP is large relative to fixed assets, the ending-
denominator ratio reads *better* than the eventual deployed base warrants, because
the capital already committed is not in the denominator.

Judge materiality by **consequence, not by a raw CIP cutoff**. For each window year
compute both `revenue / fixed assets` and `revenue / (fixed assets + CIP)`. If the
two land on opposite sides of the investment-efficiency threshold (see
`formula-profiles.md` for the 2-vs-3 bars) in any year, CIP distorts that company's
efficiency call for that year. Report both ratios and the affected years rather than
a yes/no with no arithmetic behind it. A company can be distorted under the strict
bar (3) but not the narrative bar (2), or vice versa — the two profiles are judged
independently.
