# HR Review Reconciliation

Use this method when reviewing employee matters across medical evidence,
operational records, wage entries, communications and employment procedures.
Read [evidence and chronology](references/evidence-and-chronology.md) before
adopting conclusions, and [decision and delivery checks](references/decision-and-delivery.md)
before constructing the final deliverables.

Build an inventory of the supplied sources and the requested decision scope.
Use stable employee identifiers to join records. Record each source's period,
version, author or system, and what it can actually establish. An index can help
locate evidence; verify decisive facts against the underlying source when its
meaning, timing or completeness is uncertain. Use installed document parsers;
when an optional command is unavailable, use a supported equivalent.

For each employee, create a chronology separating the event date, evidence date,
entry date, approval date and review date. Identify the applicable rule and its
conditions from the supplied policy. Evaluate the conditions for the particular
event rather than importing a different employee's stage or present-day facts.
Separate planned work, actual work and retrospectively corrected records.

Adopt a finding only after connecting the applicable rule to supported facts.
Keep the object, event, conclusion, reasoning and operative evidence together.
When an uncertainty affects the result, identify the missing fact, its owner,
how to obtain it and the protective interim action. Avoid treating absence of a
record as proof that an event did or did not occur.

Compute periods and money from source inputs with explicit boundary and item
definitions. Preserve each deduction, allowance, frozen item and benefit as a
separate transaction until the policy supports combining them. Distinguish a
correction amount from a final settlement and a proposed action from completion.

Give each corrective action a responsible role, executable deadline and
observable closure evidence. Link it to the finding it resolves. Put urgent
controls before irreversible actions; completion requires evidence of execution.

Use an authoritative decision ledger to construct all requested deliverables.
Reconcile the review report and workbooks by employee and substantive proposition. Reopen the files, recompute arithmetic,
check source references and verify that the final statuses agree. Preserve the
original inputs and explain unresolved differences in the submitted review.
