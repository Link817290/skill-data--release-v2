# Deliverables

Submit three Chinese, editable Office files in output/:

- 05-01_女职工保护审查表.xls (or .xlsx): review results and supporting date, amount and follow-up checks.
- 05-02_女职工保护专项审查报告.doc (or .docx): the substantive review report.
- 05-03_整改与审批清单.xls (or .xlsx): an actionable correction and approval list.

The English filenames audit.xlsx, report.docx and actions.xlsx are also accepted.
Use clear employee identification, conclusions and source references in the review,
and identifiable actions with accountable roles, timing, closure evidence and status
in the action list. Equivalent Chinese headers, layout, numbering and defensible
splitting or combining of actions are allowed. Exact row counts are not a scoring rule.
No machine-readable counterpart or internal identifier vocabulary is required.
The supplied evidence index locates sources; it is not a substitute for source interpretation.
