# Review Reasoning

## Rule authority

Which supplied document establishes validation conventions, and which governs
their application to analytical runs? Could an apparent rule instead be a
historical formula or a manually maintained comparison? A numerical helper can
express a convention, but cannot establish that it is authorized for this review.
Missing rule text remains missing even when a calculation is possible.

## Evidence and admissibility

What makes an observation part of the relevant population? Do sample identity,
replicate membership, treatment, dilution, and control relationships agree?
Would a change in an exclusion alter only one comparison or an upstream input
set shared by several conclusions?

Raw observations, stored formulas, cached results, and manual annotations carry
different kinds of evidence. Agreement between them is meaningful only when
their roles and dependencies are understood. Worksheet names and layouts may
vary; a reusable representation should follow meaning rather than position.

## Numerical conventions

Does the chosen calculation describe the quantity being reviewed? Consider
whether a threshold depends on transformed observations, a robust population
summary, or an element-level limit. Are the validity population and denominator
consistent with that choice?

Where does rounding enter the source calculation chain? A display-rounded result
is not necessarily interchangeable with an intermediate rounded before further
calculation. Which convention would explain a disagreement, and is that
convention supported by the governing evidence?

For suitability limits, what establishes direction, degrees of freedom, and the
tail convention of the critical value? A supplied critical value should have
its own traceable basis. The tool does not infer that convention from a label.

## Paired and curve-derived quantities

Are treated observations paired with the appropriate untreated controls? Does
the quality of the underlying replicates support using the resulting statistic
in a decision? An available numerical result need not be an admissible result.

For a titer estimate, do the selected neighboring dilution points bracket the
threshold? Does the method support the interpolation scale being considered?
Curve selection and replication quality remain distinct from interpolation.
An unbracketed or flat segment does not establish a unique interpolated titer.

## Analytical-run judgment

What separates an element-level exception from a run-level failure? Consider
whether the applicable criteria depend on validity, class-specific counts,
overall counts, ordering, or multiple conditions together. Neither an isolated
outlier nor the absence of a visible exception establishes the complete result.

What does a manual rejection or exclusion actually assert? Its effect on the
source calculation and its support as an objective disposition are separate
questions. A counterfactual comparison can clarify the impact without silently
replacing the source's recorded state.

Does a titer variability statistic have an explicit acceptance role, or is it
only descriptive? Missing acceptance criteria should remain visible as an
unresolved issue rather than being supplied by intuition.

## Reconciliation and closure

Which downstream quantities inherit a historical factor, and which rest on
independent inputs? An impact claim is stronger when its boundary follows actual
dependencies rather than a shared metric name.

Could another reviewer distinguish numerical agreement, substantive difference,
missing comparison evidence, and an undecidable disposition? Does the inventory
reconcile with the reviewed units, and do the saved conclusions retain their
evidence and uncertainty? A review is not closed by the existence of an
intermediate calculation alone.
