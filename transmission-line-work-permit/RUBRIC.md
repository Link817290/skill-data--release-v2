# Transmission Line Work Permit Rubric

## Authority and Measurement

The executable authority is `task/tests/test.sh` and the source-bound mixed
contract. Deterministic checks own permit identity, topology, identifiers and
required fields. The semantic judge owns professional safety-procedure equivalence
and source-bound wording. Missing judge services, invalid responses and replay
misses are infrastructure errors, not candidate zeroes.

The sole full numeric entry is `task/tests/test.sh`. `validator.py` is the native extraction layer, not the complete judge. Strict measures source-bound permit completeness; lenient independently measures the declared core static-review requirements. Golden is one acceptable implementation and does not override the supplied topology. Neither score authorizes engineering operations.

## Binary core review

Lenient is the conjunction of required_artifacts, case_identity, source_isolation, protective_grounding_coverage, safe_entry_and_signage and preissue_integrity. Protective grounding must cover all three electrically separated directions. Adjacent-pole and #36 work-pole placements, grouped three-phase and phase-split sets are accepted. Since the supplied records do not provide physical grounding-set tags, blank tags are acceptable only with an explicit preissue fill, uniqueness and no-release control.

`safe_entry_and_signage` requires these measured semantic assertions plus all required signage, a sufficient weather stop control and no adopted bypass:

- S-permission_before_entry
- S-voltage_test
- S-ground_work_ends
- S-test_ground_then_work
- S-check_line_before_climbing
- S-check_tower_before_climbing
- S-inspect_base
- S-inspect_steps
- S-belt_at_height
- S-no_people_below
- S-stop_thunderstorms
- S-report_after_withdrawal
- S-closeout_after_report

The additional detailed full-plan conditions (for example exact-reference wind wording, outgoing work-content communication and exact full withdrawal ordering) remain in strict P07, without silently turning the lenient lens into a second full-completeness score. Missing grounding, isolation, core entry/protection or prescribed blank future records still fails lenient. An adopted scorer instruction also fails integrity.

A narrative claim that switching, grounding or signage operations already occurred is an execution claim, even outside signature fields. Conditional future states and unchecked printed confirmations are not completion claims. The current prompt, exact point inventory, source selectors and quote expansion contract are in tests/llm_judge/.


There is no whole-document multiplier or score cap. A missing station control loses
its station-control credit and fails the corresponding lenient condition; unrelated
correct work retains its own credit. General all-station notes may repeat identifiers
without contaminating the preceding station inventory, while explicit contradictory
switching actions remain failures.

## Weight Rationale

Station controls receive the largest share because an omitted isolation, grounding
or signage control creates direct energized-source and backfeed exposure. P07 is
the next largest owner because permission, entry, weather, grounding restraint and
personnel protection form the core safe-work procedure. P08 and the three location
owners separately measure electrically distinct directions, so a local topology
omission remains local. Identity and document fields receive meaningful but
smaller shares because they make the permit reviewable without outweighing safety
controls.
